updated: 2026-08-18T12:34

- (decision) Current pain and desired behavior: slides cannot currently be added or deleted. Provide an easy button for slide creation; typing a Markdown divider (---) should automatically create a slide boundary. Arrow keys should make slide navigation easy. When the caret is at the top of the current slide and text scrolls upward, the scroll should continue into the previous slide.
- (decision) Add Slide inserts a new blank slide immediately after the current slide.
- (decision) Delete Slide acts immediately on the current slide and provides a visible Undo action rather than a confirmation dialog.
- (decision) The Markdown source remains canonical and keeps visible slide separators; typing a separator creates the next slide while preserving portable Markdown.
- (correction) Correction: the Markdown source remains canonical and keeps visible three-dash slide separators; typing a separator creates the next slide while preserving portable Markdown.
- (decision) When editing text, Up at the first line of a slide moves the caret to the end of the previous slide; Down at the last line moves it to the start of the next slide. Normal arrow behavior remains unchanged away from slide boundaries.
- (decision) Primary slide controls live in context: a small Add button between slides inserts after the preceding slide, and Delete appears on the active slide.
- (decision) After deleting a slide, focus moves to the slide that takes its place; deleting slide 2 of 3 activates the former slide 3.
- (decision) Deleting the only remaining slide clears its content but preserves one blank slide, so a presentation never has zero slides.
