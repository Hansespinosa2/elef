# Northstar Launch

From a quiet idea to a product people remember.

---

# The opportunity

People do not need more dashboards. They need a clear next step.

- Replace scattered signals with one useful view
- Make progress visible to the whole team
- Turn weekly reporting into weekly learning

---

# What changes

| Before | With Northstar |
| --- | --- |
| Five disconnected tools | One shared starting point |
| Status updates by hand | Signals collected automatically |
| Activity without direction | Decisions tied to outcomes |

---

# The launch plan

1. **Invite** a small group of design partners
2. **Observe** where the workflow breaks
3. **Refine** the moments that create momentum
4. **Expand** when the experience feels inevitable

---

# The promise

> Every team should know what matters next.

Northstar is the calm layer between information and action.
